About the Greenhouse

Greenhouse is a modern plant studio designed to help every gardener bring calm, color, and cleaner air into their home.

Our mission

We believe every space should feel alive. That’s why we combine thoughtful greenhouse care with easy, accessible guidance for both new plant owners and seasoned growers.

From selecting the right varieties to sharing seasonal care tips, our goal is to make growing plants simple, sustainable, and delightful.

What we do

  • Curated flower collections for every room
  • One-on-one plant recommendations
  • Ongoing care support and community resources

Every plant is grown with attention in our greenhouse and prepared for safe delivery to your door.
