Choosing the right flowers

Start with your light conditions: bright, indirect light for orchids and lilies, and partial shade for begonias. Consider how much daily care you can give, and choose a variety that fits your schedule.

Care essentials

  • Water on a regular schedule without overwatering.
  • Keep plants away from direct drafts and heaters.
  • Rotate containers occasionally for even growth.
